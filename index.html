02 / RESEARCH FIELDS

Three connected lines of inquiry.
Updated September 2026.

01 — PRIMARY FOCUS / EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH

Compositional learning

Can a small set of reusable computational components explain a larger family of tasks? I explore this question through synthetic task environments, modular models and held-out compositions. A central concern is distinguishing accurate predictions from recovery of the underlying mechanism.

02 — SECONDARY FOCUS / RESEARCH IN DEVELOPMENT

Efficient 3D representations

How much computation does a useful scene representation need? My graphics work explores 3D Gaussian Splatting, novel-view synthesis and convergence behavior. Current work includes experimental comparisons and manuscript development; broader evaluation remains in progress.

03 — QUANTUM COMPUTING / SIMULATION STUDY

Quantum annealing & tunneling

How does the structure of an optimization landscape affect the behavior of thermal and quantum-inspired search? My Max-Cut project explores Ising formulations and classical simulations of quantum annealing, including path-integral Monte Carlo. I am interested in the role of tunneling and the conditions under which different methods succeed. This is simulation-based work, with no claim of quantum hardware advantage.
