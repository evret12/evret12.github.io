I am Elton Liu, a high school student researcher. I am drawn to problems where mathematics, physics, and computation meet: learning structure from data, searching hard optimization landscapes, and representing three-dimensional scenes efficiently.

Mathematical modeling is one of my strongest tools. I like reducing a complicated problem to its essential variables, building a testable model, and checking the result against evidence. I also learn new technical material quickly by working from first principles, then turning it into a small experiment or prototype.

02 / RESEARCH

Three connected research directions.

01 — PRIMARY FOCUS

Compositional learning

Can a small set of reusable computational components explain a larger family of tasks? I explore this through synthetic task environments, modular models, and held-out compositions.

02 — RESEARCH IN DEVELOPMENT

Efficient 3D representations

My graphics work explores 3D Gaussian Splatting, novel-view synthesis, and convergence behavior. Current work includes experimental comparisons and manuscript development.

03 — QUANTUM COMPUTING / SIMULATION STUDY

Quantum annealing & tunneling

My Max-Cut project explores Ising formulations and classical simulations of quantum annealing, including path-integral Monte Carlo. I am interested in tunneling, energy landscapes, and search methods.

03 / WORKING STYLE

Understand first.
Then test the idea.

Mathematical modeling

I identify the key assumptions, variables, and limits of a problem before implementing a model.

Fast technical learning

I move quickly through unfamiliar material by connecting formal ideas to small examples and working code.

Working prototypes

I turn questions into Python experiments, controlled simulations, and model comparisons.

Careful evaluation

I use baselines, ablations, and held-out settings to test a conclusion.
